How to read this

Exploratory questions are people learning about the category. A low score means you're not seen as a source of explanation on your own subject.

Evaluative questions are people choosing. A low score here is the expensive one — it means you're not in the consideration set at the moment the decision gets made.

Brand questions are people checking you specifically. A low score means the models don't have a clear picture of who you are, which usually points at a structural problem rather than a content one.

Content and authority gaps

Question Who was cited Gap type
[question] [source] No content

The three kinds of gap

No content. Nothing exists on the topic. The fix is to build it.

Not citable. Content exists but says nothing a model can attribute to you — no data, no distinct claim, nothing that isn't already said elsewhere. The fix is to add something only you could have produced.

Wrong structure. The content is good but the page can't be parsed — no direct answer, no clear headings, no attribution. The fix is cheap and fast.
